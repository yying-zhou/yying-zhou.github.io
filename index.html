RESEARCH INTERESTS

Spatial Representation & Environmental Perception

How do different modes of spatial representation shape which aspects of the environment people notice, value, and act upon? I'm interested in how maps, 3D models, and immersive environments each reveal or obscure different qualities, particularly ecological features that are marginal in traditional plan views: vertical structure, understory habitats, sensory atmospheres.

Participatory Methods & Multispecies Planning

I explore how participatory spatial tools can be designed to include non-human values in urban planning. This involves developing methods for eliciting and analyzing place-based ecological values, and designing representational formats that make these values understandable to different stakeholders (planners, residents, ecologists).

Geospatial Technology & Design Research

I investigate how emerging spatial technologies can lower barriers to community-engaged spatial research. My focus is on 3D reconstruction, generative models, and web-based GIS as open-source tools that support both rigorous inquiry and practical application in planning contexts.
