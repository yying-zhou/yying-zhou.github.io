03 · 2018 · Design Project

Abracadabra

Landscape Design / Under-viaduct Activation

Abracadabra activates an under-viaduct area in Corvetto, Milan, where sidewalks are neglected and people move through as mere passersby. While Corvetto is currently stifled by vehicular dominance and spatial fragmentation, these very 'voids' under the infrastructure offer a latent opportunity for biophilic intervention and community re-stitching. Informed by Marc Augé's concept of "non-place" and references such as Kobo Abe's "Box," the project treats public discomfort and safety as design questions.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can infrastructural leftovers shift passersby from transit-only roles into social participants with identity and agency?

KEY TAKEAWAY

Design can transform "non-places" by choreographing relationships, not only forms.
